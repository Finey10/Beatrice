PUT HER SONG HERE ✦

1. Add the song file and name it exactly:  song.mp3
   (so the full path is  audio/song.mp3)

2. Open js/config.js and set:
     SONG_NAME: "Yellow — Coldplay"   (or whatever your song is)

Notes
- The song NEVER plays by itself. She presses play.
- If your file is .m4a or .wav, either convert it to mp3
  (e.g. cloudconvert.com) or change AUDIO_FILE in js/config.js
  to "audio/song.m4a".
- Until a file exists, the player shows a small note instead of
  an error. Nothing breaks.
